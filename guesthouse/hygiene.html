    Welcome in.A small ritual before you roll your suitcase through the door

    Two quick habits keep the floors clean for every guest who stays after you — here's exactly how, and where to find everything you need.

    Step 0 · Find the kit

    Everything's on the shoe cabinet

    Right at the entrance, on top of the shoe cabinet, you'll find a small bag of wet wipes and a set of fabric wheel covers — put there just for this.

    • ✓Wipe your suitcase wheels with the provided wet wipes
    • ✓Slip on the wheel covers before rolling inside
    • ✓Both items live on top of the shoe cabinet, entrance side
    Walkthrough

    Four steps, thirty seconds

    A quick wipe and cover for every wheel, every time you come in.

    1

    Grab a wipe

    Reach for the wet wipes waiting by the entrance, right next to where your suitcase stands.

    2

    Wipe each wheel

    Give every wheel a quick clean, working around the corners where road dust collects.

    3

    Stretch on a cover

    Pull a fabric wheel cover snugly over each freshly wiped wheel.

    4

    Roll on in

    All four wheels wiped and covered — you're all set to bring your suitcase inside.

    Thank you for keeping it spotless

    It's a small step that makes a real difference for housekeeping and for the next guest.

    Enjoy your stay
