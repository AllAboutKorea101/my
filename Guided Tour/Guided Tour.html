Why you'll love this hike

  • Watch the sky turn gold over Seoul from a ridge most tourists never find
  • Step into Gyeongwonjeongsa Temple's quiet courtyards with someone who knows its story
  • Talk real history, culture, and daily life with your guide the whole way up — no script
  • End the night with real Korean food (and better stories) at a cozy local spot

About this experience

This isn't a sightseeing checklist — it's an evening with a local friend who happens to know every hidden staircase, temple bell, and noodle shop on Achasan.

Climb through quiet pine forest as the city turns gold below you, along the same ridge line BTS's RM and V once hiked on Run BTS. Stop at Gyeongwonjeongsa Temple for a moment among centuries-old rooftops, then head back down to a warm, unpretentious meal your guide actually eats. Expect real conversation about Korea along the way — not a rehearsed script.
